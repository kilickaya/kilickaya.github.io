# Patent concept illustrations

Four editorial SVG drawings using the Research illustration system: 800 × 500 canvases, oat backgrounds, paper surfaces, fine charcoal outlines and muted sage/terracotta accents. They illustrate the central ideas rather than literal patented architectures or performance results.

- `conversational-search.svg`: a visual query and conversational refinement guide image search. [Original patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2023117041A1/).
- `object-localization.svg`: human cues guide localization of an interacted object. [Original patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20220414371A1/).
- `interaction-recognition.svg`: subject and object branches meet in an interaction representation. [Original patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11481576B2/).
- `context-learning.svg`: scene context informs learning about a human-object pair. [Original patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP4058930A1/).

Regenerate alongside the publication drawings with `python3 scripts/draw-publications.py`. The site preserves original titles, inventors, citation labels and links without adding explanatory paragraphs. Patent status and dates have not been reinterpreted.
